## Findings to fix

1. Pasted secrets: never quote any part, even to say it is weak.
2. "Do not ask me about each one" shortens the questions to one table; it does not remove the table.
3. Post writer: durations, temperatures and numbers get a `[please check]` marker; no meta text ("I will add links") inside the post body; ask for an explicit yes before publishing.
4. Style cards and style guides: anything inferred from absence is marked as an assumption.
5. Comment replies are written in the language of the comment.
6. SEO audit: do not promise results "within days".
7. Social promotion: stay with the points of the article or mark additions.
8. Speed audit: order the list by impact.
